🚀 How to Run & Install the App

  1. In Google AI Studio Build: View and test the live app directly in your browser using the built-in Streaming Android Emulator.
  2. On Your Android Device: In AI Studio, open the project menu → select Export → click Generate APK. Download the .apk file to your Android phone and tap to install.
  3. In Android Studio (Local PC): Clone this GitHub repository to your computer, open it in Android Studio, and click Run (Shift + F10) on an emulator or physical device.

🛠️ 3. Troubleshooting Common Playback & Export Issues

Audio is out of sync with the anime character's mouth movements. How to fix? ▼
AI Auto-Fix Steps:
1. In the Studio or Voice Studio screen, tap the Audio Voice Trim tool.
2. Adjust the dual seek bar to align the start and end points.
3. Go to Settings > App Settings > Audio Vocal Buffer and select "High Fidelity (Low Latency)".
4. Ensure your device speech engine (Google Speech Services) is updated from the Play Store.
Video playback shows a black screen or stutters on my mobile device. ▼
Instant Solution:
• In Export Presets, choose 720p HD at 30 fps instead of 4K 60fps for devices with less than 4GB RAM.
• Clear the temporary render cache via Settings > Storage > Clear Render Buffer.
• Restart playback in the Anime Player screen; the app will synthesize using standard hardware accelerated codecs.
Video export freezes or cannot save to device gallery. ▼
Fix via DocumentFile Storage Access Framework:
On Android 10+, apps cannot write directly to raw file paths without permission. In the export dialog, tap "Choose Storage Directory (SAF)" and select your Movies or Downloads folder. The app will write cleanly using the Android DocumentFile API.

📤 How to Export Your Projects & Share to Social Media

Step-by-step instructions to export generated anime videos, choose optimal aspect ratios, and share directly to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.

Fast sharing with embedded audio and SRT subtitle tracks

  1. Select Aspect Ratio in Project Settings:
    Open the project and go to Project Settings or tap the Aspect Ratio chip. Select 9:16 (TikTok/Shorts), 16:9 (YouTube), or 1:1 (Instagram).
  2. Configure Resolution & Frame Rate:
    Navigate to Export Presets. Choose between 720p HD, 1080p Full HD, or 4K Ultra, and select 30 fps, 60 fps, or 90 fps anime frame interpolation.
  3. Apply Real-Time Voice Mood Filters:
    Before rendering, use the Voice Mood Filter Slider in the Audio Editor to apply Deep Bass, High Pitch, or Robot filters to character dialogue.
  4. Export via DocumentFile SAF to Public Storage:
    Click Export Video. When prompted, select your preferred device folder (e.g. Movies/AnimeStudio) to save the finished .mp4 file directly into your phone gallery using Android's secure DocumentFile API.
  5. One-Tap Share to Social Media:
    Once rendering reaches 100%, tap Share Project. Choose your destination app (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, WhatsApp, or X/Twitter). The app automatically includes your anime title, generated tags, and video file ready to post!
